# Third-party components and licensing notes

The initial prototype uses Qt 6 modules:
- Qt Widgets
- Qt Multimedia
- Qt Multimedia Widgets
- Qt SVG

The application source is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Qt licensing depends on the selected Qt distribution and applicable open-source license. Verify notices and redistribution terms for the exact Qt release used.

Future work may add FFmpeg, codec libraries, OpenTimelineIO, OpenFX, AI models, or other components. Each addition requires a compatibility, patent, source-disclosure, attribution, and redistribution review. Do not assume a codec or plugin can be redistributed merely because it can be used locally.
